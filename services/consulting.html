Small Business Consulting Services

What we deliver for small businesses.

🔍
Business Operations Assessment

We review your current workflows, systems, and processes end-to-end, then deliver a structured report identifying what's working, what's costing you time and money, and the highest-leverage improvements to make first. The starting point for most ColeHart engagements.

⚙️
Workflow & Process Improvement

We map where work slows down, where tasks repeat unnecessarily, and where better structure can save time. Then we help you redesign the process so it runs consistently without constant oversight and without everything depending on one person.

📋
Process & SOP Documentation

We help document your key workflows, responsibilities, checklists, and repeatable processes so your business can run more consistently, train new people faster, and reduce owner dependency. If it only exists in someone's head, it's a risk.

🤖
AI & Automation Integration

We identify where AI, automation, and better tools can save time, reduce manual work, and improve how your business operates. The goal isn't AI for its own sake it's to give your team back capacity and reduce dependence on any one person in your operation.

🏗️
Organizational Systems Design

Build the internal processes, documentation, and operating systems that allow your business to run and grow without depending entirely on you. Designed for business owners who want to scale without losing their sanity or their family time.

How We Work

Structured, collaborative, and transparent.

1
Discovery first.
Every engagement begins with discovery. We spend time understanding your environment before we make recommendations because the wrong diagnosis leads to the wrong solution.
2
Collaborative delivery.
Delivery is structured, documented, and collaborative. Your team stays involved throughout the process, so you're not just handed a report at the end. We build internal capability alongside every deliverable.
3
Transparent pricing.
Pricing is scope-based and transparent. No retainers required to start. We define the engagement scope clearly before any work begins, so you know exactly what you're getting and there are no surprises.
