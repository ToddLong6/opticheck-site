Who We Are

About ColeHart Enterprises LLC

ColeHart Enterprises LLC is a South Carolina-based, veteran-owned small business providing records cleanup, workflow documentation, project admin support, data cleanup, and digital forms support.

We help agencies, schools, nonprofits, prime contractors, and small businesses organize information, document processes, track deliverables, clean up operational data, and modernize paper or email-based workflows.

Our Background

ColeHart was built from a foundation of military service, operations leadership, project coordination, and practical technology experience.

Our work is focused on structure, documentation, follow-through, and useful deliverables. Whether the need is a records inventory, SOP, project tracker, cleaned spreadsheet, or digital intake form, our goal is to help teams bring order to the work that slows them down.

How We Work

We keep the work practical, organized, and deliverable-focused.

We identify what needs cleanup, organize the information, document the process, and leave the client with usable files, trackers, SOPs, reports, forms, or dashboards.
