Company Overview

ColeHart Enterprises LLC provides operations consulting, technology support, AI readiness, training, and project coordination services for public-sector organizations, schools, nonprofits, and businesses. We help organizations identify workflow gaps, improve internal processes, document procedures, evaluate technology needs, and train teams to work more efficiently.
