About ColeHart Enterprises

Who we are.

ColeHart Enterprises LLC is a professional services and business support firm that helps public-sector organizations, schools, nonprofits, and businesses improve operations, modernize workflows, support procurement needs, and train staff. We focus on practical solutions that help organizations reduce inefficiencies, improve coordination, adopt technology, and complete projects more effectively.
