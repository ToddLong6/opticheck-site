Government Opportunity Tracking
Opticbid

A government opportunity tracking tool built to help small businesses find and review contract opportunities. Scan federal and state opportunities, filter by your NAICS codes, and review matches before your workday starts.

What It Does
  • Scans federal and state contract opportunities
  • Filters by NAICS codes and certifications
  • Scores and ranks opportunities by fit
  • Delivers a prioritized opportunity briefing
  • Helps identify bid targets before competitors
Who It's For
  • Small businesses pursuing government contracts
  • Veteran-owned businesses
  • Prime contractors building pipeline
  • New GovCon entrants learning the market
  • Teams without a dedicated BD function
Key Features
  • Opportunity scanning and scoring
  • NAICS and certification matching
  • Daily opportunity briefings
  • Federal and state coverage
  • Free trial available
